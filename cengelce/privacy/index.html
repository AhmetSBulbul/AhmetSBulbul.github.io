Çengelce Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Çengelce mobile app (the “App”) processes, why and for how long, and how you can exercise your rights. If the Turkish and English versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

1. Data controller

Ahmet Safa Bülbül
Email: ahmetsafabulbul@gmail.com

2. No sign-up required

The App does not ask for your email, phone number, password or real name. On first launch an anonymous account is created automatically for your device. It is just a random identifier tied to that device. If you delete the App, you cannot get back into this account.

3. Data we process and why

DataPurposeLegal basis
Anonymous account ID and session data Linking your diamond balance and username to your device Performance of a contract
Username (optional, chosen by you) Identifying your account; showing it on leaderboards in the future Performance of a contract
Diamond transactions: amount, reason (e.g. revealing a letter, ad reward), time, ad transaction ID Keeping your balance correct across reinstalls and preventing abuse Performance of a contract; legitimate interests
Puzzle reports: the puzzle and clue reported, the issue you picked, your note (up to 500 characters), app version Fixing wrong content Legitimate interests
Technical logs: IP address, request time, device and app version Running the service, security, rate limiting Legitimate interests
Advertising data (Google AdMob): advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactions Showing optional rewarded ads and verifying the reward Consent where required; otherwise legitimate interests

Please do not put personal information in the puzzle report note.

4. Data that stays on your device

Your game progress, unfinished games, statistics, settings and downloaded puzzles are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. “İlerlemeyi sıfırla” (Reset progress) in Settings, or deleting the App, removes them.

5. Advertising

The App shows only rewarded ads that you start yourself, through Google AdMob. In regions such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad. You can change your choice under Ayarlar (Settings) › “Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri” (Ad privacy options). If you do not consent to personalised ads, ads are shown without personalisation. On iOS the App does not request access to the advertising identifier (IDFA).

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

6. Service providers and transfers

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): hosts the account, username, diamond transactions and reports. Servers are in the EU, in Sweden (eu-north-1).
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.): ad serving and reward verification.

These providers are outside Türkiye, so data is transferred abroad in line with applicable law. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone other than the providers above, except where required by law.

7. Retention

  • Account ID, username and diamond transactions: until the account is deleted. If an account is not used for 24 months, these records are deleted.
  • Puzzle reports: up to 12 months after receipt.
  • Server technical logs: for the provider’s retention period, typically a few days.
  • Advertising data: according to Google’s own policy.

8. Your rights and deletion requests

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing and withdraw consent. Send your request to ahmetsafabulbul@gmail.com together with your username so we can find your account. We reply within 30 days. If you never chose a username, your account cannot be linked to you; deleting the App is enough, and server records are deleted within the periods in section 7. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

The App is for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always at this address and the effective date above changes. We announce important changes in the App.
